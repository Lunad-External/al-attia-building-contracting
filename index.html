Who We Are

Al Attia Group provides comprehensive engineering, contracting and building solutions, catering to the diverse requirements of residential and commercial properties across the UAE.

With a focus on quality workmanship, practical solutions and customer satisfaction, we offer a broad range of services spanning HVAC installation and maintenance, aluminium and facade works, building maintenance, interior fit-out, kitchen solutions and property renovation.

From maintaining efficient air conditioning systems to transforming interiors and undertaking building improvement works, we tailor every solution to the requirements of the project, whether it is a private villa, an apartment, an office or a commercial building.

Based in Ajman, our team brings together contracting and maintenance capabilities with in-house manufacturing for aluminium works, kitchens and furniture. This allows us to coordinate design, production and installation closely, keep communication clear from the first site visit to handover, and support clients with reliable aftercare.
